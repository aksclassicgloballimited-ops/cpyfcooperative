## Subtitle / short description

Manage cooperative savings, shares, loans and membership updates.

## Full description

CPYIF Cooperative gives members a mobile way to access their Circle of Prosperous Youth Interest-Free Cooperative account.

Members can:

- View membership status and account information.
- Submit weekly savings payments for cooperative review.
- Request share units and follow approval status.
- View cooperative notifications and membership updates.
- Access loan applications and account services available to their membership.

An internet connection is required to sign in and use account services. Account balances and financial records are not stored for offline access. Push notifications are optional and can be disabled in device settings.

CPYIF is a registered cooperative society (RC No. 7379803). Membership and financial services are subject to cooperative rules and eligibility requirements.
